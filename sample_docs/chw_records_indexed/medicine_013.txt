MEDICINE: Aspirin
CATEGORY: Analgesic / Antiplatelet
INDICATIONS: Pain, fever (adults only). Antiplatelet: post-MI, stroke prevention, cardiovascular disease.
ADULT DOSE: Pain/fever: 300–600mg every 4–6 hours. Antiplatelet: 75–100mg once daily.
CHILD DOSE: NOT recommended under 16 years — risk of Reye's syndrome (fatal liver and brain damage).
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Children under 16. Peptic ulcer. Bleeding disorders. Anticoagulant therapy.
NOTES: Never give to children or teenagers with viral illness. Critical contraindication.
