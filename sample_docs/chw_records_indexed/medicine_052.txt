MEDICINE: Sodium Cromoglicate (Eye Drops)
CATEGORY: Mast Cell Stabiliser Eye Drop
INDICATIONS: Allergic conjunctivitis prevention. Seasonal eye allergies.
ADULT DOSE: 1–2 drops four times daily.
CHILD DOSE (over 2 years): Same as adult.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Known hypersensitivity.
NOTES: Preventive — must be used regularly before and during allergy season. Less effective for acute symptoms than Ketotifen.
