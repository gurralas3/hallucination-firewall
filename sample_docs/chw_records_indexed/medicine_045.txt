MEDICINE: Ethambutol
CATEGORY: Antituberculosis
INDICATIONS: Tuberculosis (combination — first 2 months).
ADULT DOSE: 15mg/kg once daily.
CHILD DOSE: 20mg/kg once daily (maximum 1g).
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Optic neuritis. Poor visual acuity. Children too young for visual monitoring.
NOTES: Can cause optic neuritis — check vision before and during treatment. Stop if visual changes occur.
