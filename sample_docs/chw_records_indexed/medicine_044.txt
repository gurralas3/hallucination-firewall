MEDICINE: Pyrazinamide
CATEGORY: Antituberculosis
INDICATIONS: Tuberculosis (first 2 months of standard regimen only).
ADULT DOSE: 25mg/kg once daily. Maximum 2g/day.
CHILD DOSE: 30–40mg/kg once daily (maximum 2g).
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Severe liver disease. Acute gout.
NOTES: Used only in first 2 months of TB treatment. Causes hyperuricaemia — can trigger gout.
