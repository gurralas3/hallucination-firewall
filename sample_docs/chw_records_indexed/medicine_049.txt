MEDICINE: Ketotifen (Eye Drops)
CATEGORY: Antihistamine Eye Drop
INDICATIONS: Allergic conjunctivitis — itchy, watery, red eyes due to allergy. Hay fever eye symptoms.
ADULT DOSE: 1 drop in affected eye(s) twice daily.
CHILD DOSE (over 3 years): 1 drop twice daily.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Known hypersensitivity to ketotifen. Not for contact lens wearers during use.
NOTES: First-line WHO-recommended treatment for allergic conjunctivitis. Treats the allergic cause — not just redness. Different from vasoconstrictors like Tetrahydrozoline (Visine) which only reduce redness without treating allergy.
