MEDICINE: Haloperidol
CATEGORY: Antipsychotic
INDICATIONS: Schizophrenia. Acute psychosis. Severe agitation. Nausea and vomiting (low dose).
ADULT DOSE: Psychosis: 2–10mg two to three times daily. Acute agitation: 5–10mg IM.
CHILD DOSE: Not recommended under 3 years. Age 3–12: 0.025–0.05mg/kg/day.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Parkinson's disease. Coma. QT prolongation.
NOTES: Extrapyramidal side effects common — rigidity, tremor. Give anticholinergic if needed.
