MEDICINE: Lamivudine (3TC)
CATEGORY: Antiretroviral
INDICATIONS: HIV treatment (combination regimen). Hepatitis B.
ADULT DOSE: HIV: 150mg twice daily or 300mg once daily.
CHILD DOSE: 4mg/kg twice daily (maximum 150mg per dose).
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Known hypersensitivity.
NOTES: Well-tolerated. Core component of most WHO first-line HIV regimens. Also active against Hepatitis B.
