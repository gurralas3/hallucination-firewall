MEDICINE: Amoxicillin-Clavulanate (Co-Amoxiclav)
CATEGORY: Antibiotic
INDICATIONS: Infections resistant to plain Amoxicillin — sinusitis, diabetic foot, animal bites, skin abscesses.
ADULT DOSE: 625mg every 8 hours or 1g every 12 hours for 5–7 days.
CHILD DOSE: 40mg/kg/day of amoxicillin component in divided doses every 8 hours.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Penicillin allergy. Liver disease. Prior jaundice from this medicine.
NOTES: Stronger than plain Amoxicillin. Higher GI side effects — take with food.
