MEDICINE: Artesunate (IV)
CATEGORY: Antimalarial
INDICATIONS: Severe Plasmodium falciparum malaria — the only WHO-recommended treatment for severe malaria.
ADULT DOSE: 2.4mg/kg IV at 0h, 12h, 24h, then once daily.
CHILD DOSE (under 20kg): 3mg/kg IV at same intervals.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Known hypersensitivity to artemisinins.
NOTES: Replaces quinine as first-line for severe malaria per WHO 2023 guidelines. Oral treatment must follow IV course.
