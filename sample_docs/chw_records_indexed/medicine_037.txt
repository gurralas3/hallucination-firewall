MEDICINE: Mebendazole
CATEGORY: Anthelmintic
INDICATIONS: Intestinal worms — roundworm, whipworm, hookworm, pinworm.
ADULT DOSE: 100mg twice daily for 3 days OR 500mg single dose.
CHILD DOSE (over 1 year): Same as adult.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Under 1 year. Pregnancy.
NOTES: Alternative to Albendazole. Less absorbed — acts locally in gut. Safe and well-tolerated.
