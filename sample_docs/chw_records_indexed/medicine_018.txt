MEDICINE: Vitamin A
CATEGORY: Nutritional
INDICATIONS: Vitamin A deficiency. Prevention of complications in measles. Night blindness.
CHILD DOSE (under 6 months): 50,000 IU once.
CHILD DOSE (6–11 months): 100,000 IU once.
CHILD DOSE (12 months and above): 200,000 IU once, repeat after 4 weeks in deficiency.
ADULT DOSE: 200,000 IU for deficiency.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Hypervitaminosis A. Pregnancy — high doses teratogenic.
NOTES: Give immediately to children with measles in areas of deficiency. Reduces measles mortality.
