MEDICINE: Ciprofloxacin
CATEGORY: Antibiotic
INDICATIONS: Urinary tract infections, gastrointestinal infections, respiratory infections, anthrax exposure.
ADULT DOSE: 250–500mg twice daily for 3–7 days depending on indication.
CHILD DOSE: Not routinely recommended in children — use only under medical supervision. 10–15mg/kg twice daily if essential.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Hypersensitivity to fluoroquinolones. Caution in children and adolescents — affects cartilage development.
NOTES: Not first-line for simple UTI. Avoid antacids within 2 hours of dose.
