MEDICINE: Tetrahydrozoline (Visine / Eye Drops)
CATEGORY: Vasoconstrictor Eye Drop
INDICATIONS: Temporary relief of eye redness only.
ADULT DOSE: 1–2 drops up to 4 times daily. Maximum 3 days use.
CHILD DOSE: Not recommended under 6 years.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Narrow-angle glaucoma. Chronic use — causes rebound redness.
NOTES: Treats redness only — does NOT treat itching, allergy, or infection. Rebound hyperaemia with overuse. Not appropriate for allergic conjunctivitis — use Ketotifen instead.
