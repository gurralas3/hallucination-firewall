MEDICINE: Oxytocin
CATEGORY: Uterotonic
INDICATIONS: Prevention and treatment of postpartum haemorrhage (PPH). Induction of labour.
PPH PREVENTION DOSE: 10 IU intramuscularly immediately after delivery of baby.
PPH TREATMENT DOSE: 20–40 IU in 1 litre IV fluid, infuse at rate to control bleeding.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Uterine hyperstimulation. Fetal distress before delivery.
NOTES: First-line drug for PPH prevention. Most critical medicine in preventing maternal death. Keep refrigerated.
