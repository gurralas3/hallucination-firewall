MEDICINE: Rifampicin
CATEGORY: Antituberculosis
INDICATIONS: Tuberculosis (always in combination). Leprosy. Meningococcal prophylaxis.
ADULT DOSE (TB): 600mg once daily or 10mg/kg. Part of standard RHEZ regimen.
CHILD DOSE: 10–20mg/kg once daily (maximum 600mg).
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Liver disease. Concurrent protease inhibitor use.
NOTES: Colours urine, saliva, sweat orange-red — warn patients. Powerful enzyme inducer — many drug interactions including contraceptives and ARVs.
