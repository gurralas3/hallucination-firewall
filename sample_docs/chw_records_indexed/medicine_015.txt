MEDICINE: Zinc Sulfate
CATEGORY: Nutritional / Antidiarrhoeal
INDICATIONS: Adjunct treatment for childhood diarrhoea. Zinc deficiency.
CHILD DOSE (under 6 months): 10mg daily for 10–14 days.
CHILD DOSE (6 months and above): 20mg daily for 10–14 days.
ADULT DOSE: 20mg daily for zinc deficiency.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: None significant at therapeutic doses.
NOTES: Always give alongside ORS for childhood diarrhoea. Reduces duration and severity.
