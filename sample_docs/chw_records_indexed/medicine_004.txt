MEDICINE: Metronidazole
CATEGORY: Antibiotic / Antiprotozoal
INDICATIONS: Anaerobic bacterial infections, giardiasis, amoebiasis, trichomoniasis, bacterial vaginosis.
ADULT DOSE: 400–500mg three times daily for 5–7 days. Giardia: 2g single dose.
CHILD DOSE: 7.5mg/kg three times daily for 5–7 days.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: First trimester of pregnancy. Alcohol — causes severe vomiting reaction.
NOTES: Do not drink alcohol during treatment and for 48 hours after. Metallic taste is common.
