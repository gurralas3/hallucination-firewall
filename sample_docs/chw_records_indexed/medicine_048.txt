MEDICINE: Dextrose 50% (Glucose)
CATEGORY: Emergency
INDICATIONS: Hypoglycaemia (low blood sugar). Coma of unknown cause.
ADULT DOSE: 50ml of 50% dextrose IV (25g glucose). Repeat if no response.
CHILD DOSE: 2ml/kg of 10% dextrose IV.
NEONATES: 2ml/kg of 10% dextrose IV slowly.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Hyperglycaemia. Intracerebral haemorrhage (use with caution).
NOTES: Check blood glucose before administering if possible. 10% solution preferred in children — less irritating to veins.
