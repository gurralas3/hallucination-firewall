MEDICINE: Amitriptyline
CATEGORY: Antidepressant / Neuropathic pain
INDICATIONS: Depression. Neuropathic pain. Migraine prevention. Fibromyalgia.
ADULT DOSE: Depression: 25mg at night, increase to 75–150mg/day. Neuropathic pain: 10–25mg at night.
CHILD DOSE: Not recommended under 12 years for depression.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Recent myocardial infarction. Arrhythmia. MAO inhibitor use within 14 days.
NOTES: Sedating — take at night. Anticholinergic effects: dry mouth, constipation, urinary retention. Overdose is dangerous.
